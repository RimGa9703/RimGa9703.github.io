Treasure In The Dark Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026
Developer: beyondtheworldstudio

1. Information we collect

  • Google sign-in: an account identifier (ID token) issued when you sign in with Google. We do not separately store your email or name.
  • Game save data: progress, currencies, equipment and other gameplay records.
  • Advertising ID and device information: Google AdMob may collect the advertising ID, device information and approximate location (from IP) to serve ads.

2. How we use it

  • Sign-in and syncing your save data across devices
  • Serving rewarded and interstitial ads and measuring their performance
  • Finding errors and improving the game

3. Third parties

  • Google (sign-in, AdMob): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services authentication and Cloud Save): https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

We do not sell your data or share it for any other purpose. All data is transferred encrypted (HTTPS).

4. Retention and deletion

Save data is kept until you request deletion. Email us with the Google account you signed in with and we will delete your save data. Uninstalling the app does not delete server-side save data.

5. Ad settings

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

6. Children

This game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
