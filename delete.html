삭제 요청 방법

  1. rimga9703@gmail.com 으로 이메일을 보냅니다. 제목: "Treasure In The Dark 계정 삭제"

기기에 남은 데이터는 앱을 삭제하면 함께 지워집니다.

Treasure In The Dark Account and Data Deletion

Developer: Beyond The World Studio

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to rimga9703@gmail.com with the subject "Treasure In The Dark account deletion".
  2. Include the Google account email address you signed in with in the game.
  3. We will delete your data within 7 days and confirm by reply.

Data that is deleted

  • Your game account (Unity Gaming Services player ID) and its Google sign-in link
  • Game save data stored on the server (progress, currencies, equipment, etc.)

Data that is kept

  • We do not keep any other data.
  • Advertising data is handled under Google AdMob's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.
